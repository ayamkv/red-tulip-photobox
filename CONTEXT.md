# Red Tulip Glossary

## Photobox

The browser-based camera experience where visitors style, take, preview, and download a photo with the Red Tulip frame.

## Signature

A user-provided name or short message rendered as `Berdasteran with ...` on the Photobox preview and permanently composited into the downloaded photo.

## Frame Format

The selected landscape or portrait composition that determines the Photobox preview and downloaded photo dimensions.

## Multi-Photo Frame

A vertical 1080x1920 Photobox frame with three 16:9 camera slots. Each slot starts 70px from the side, the first slot starts 60px from the top, and the vertical gap between slots is 18px.

The multi-photo capture flow takes one slot at a time. After each slot is captured, the visitor can retake that slot or continue to the next one.
